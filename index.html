Episode uploader

Episode uploader is a private tool. It uploads video files to the YouTube channel of the person who runs it, sets their title, description and thumbnail, and schedules the time at which each video becomes public.

It is not a public service: it has no sign-up, and it is used only by its owner, on the owner's own channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API, with access granted by the owner of the channel through Google's consent screen. That access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account page under "Third-party apps and services".
